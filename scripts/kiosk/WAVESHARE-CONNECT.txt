WaveShare mini facial display — why Ubuntu "doesn't see" it
===========================================================

Your PC currently reports:
  HDMI-1  = connected (main panel video)
  HDMI-2  = disconnected (0-byte EDID)  ← no video signal here
  USB     = WaveShare WS170120          ← TOUCH ONLY

WaveShare HDMI LCDs need BOTH:
  1. HDMI cable from the mini panel → free HDMI/DP port on the PC
  2. USB cable from the mini panel → USB port (touch; already working)

If only USB is plugged in, Ubuntu will never show a second monitor.
Software cannot invent a second display without that HDMI/DP video link.

After both cables + power:
  sudo -E bash ~/CLARA_LAUNCH/CLARA-LAUNCH/scripts/kiosk/wire-second-display.sh
  ~/.local/bin/clara-kiosk-start
